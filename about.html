Our Story

PocketMate was born from a simple idea: financial management should be easy, visual, and stress-free. As students, we saw that tracking expenses and planning budgets did not need to be complicated.

We created PocketMate to help people take control of their money and build better habits for the future.

Our Mission

To make financial management simple, visual, and accessible, especially for students and young users.

We aim to encourage smart spending, mindful saving, and a stronger financial future for everyone.
